Portsight Photos Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Portsight Photos is a private app used by one family. It is run by its developer, who can be contacted at cuijiajun6666@gmail.com.

What Google data the app uses

The app requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it the app can only create files in, and see files and folders that the app itself created in, the Google Drive of the account that authorized it. It cannot see or change any other files, email, contacts or other Google data.

The app uses this permission only to:

  • upload photos and videos that a family member chooses in the app into a designated folder;
  • let the family's private home server download those uploaded files and then delete them from Google Drive.

Data stored on your device

The app stores its Google access credential only in the iPhone Keychain. It does not keep copies of photos beyond what the iPhone Photos library already holds.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. Photos go only to the family organizer's own Google Drive and then to the family's own private server.

Portsight Photos' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded files stay in Google Drive only until the home server collects them, normally within 15 minutes, and are then permanently deleted from Google Drive. Copies kept in the family archive can be deleted on request by contacting the email above.

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

隐私政策（中文摘要）
