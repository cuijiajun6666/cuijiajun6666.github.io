Portsight Photos

Portsight Photos is a private, family-only iPhone app. It lets members of one family send photos they choose to a folder in the family organizer's own Google Drive. A private home server owned by the same person then copies those photos into the family photo archive and removes them from Google Drive.

The app is not offered to the public, has no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics. It only uses the Google Drive permission drive.file, which allows access only to files and folders the app itself created.

Portsight Photos 是一个只给自己家人用的 iPhone App：家人选好照片，直接传到管理者本人 Google 云盘里的一个文件夹，家里的私人服务器再把照片收进家庭相册，并删掉云盘里的那份。不对外提供，没有广告，不做统计。
